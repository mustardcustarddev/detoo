# You try it first

You make a folder of adr-tools records, and run `detoo doctor --fix` on it,
with the release you built. Do none of it yet: the drill has you do it later,
if you come to it.

What you know of the reporter's case is in the issue: they installed with
brew, they ran `--fix`, and `doc/adr` is gone. You do not know which release
brew gave them, what else they ran, what their folder held, or what doctor
printed for them. Whatever you see in your folder, you cannot tell whether
it is what they saw.

Meanwhile, the issue has had no reply for an hour, and the reporter comments
again:

> FIRE DRILL: this comment is part of a fire drill.
>
> Hello? Is anyone looking at this? I still can't find our decisions.

Information 2 confirms the problem "from what the reporter sent". Go back to
step 2 of
[the drill](../../FIREDRILL-0001-serious-problem-with-a-release.md#the-drill),
and choose again.
